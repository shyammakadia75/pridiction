Notes
-----
- Read-only: production DB SHA-256 before == after load; no writes performed.
- Identical chronological test fixtures for all three models, aligned by (match_date, home_team_id, away_team_id).
- EloModel.fit was fixed in Phase 21 to remove same-day leakage: same-date fixtures are now scored against pre-date ratings and the day's updates are batched. On this dataset same-date fixtures involve disjoint teams, so the fix is correctness-hygiene with zero metric impact (reported for honesty).
- Metric caveat: Elo emits an expected home SCORE (2-class), not a calibrated 3-class probability. LR and Poisson emit proper 3-class probabilities, so their Brier/log-loss are directly comparable; Elo is compared on 3-class metrics only (accuracy/macro-F1/confusion/P-R).
- Training-regime asymmetry: LR trains on the chronological train split only (e.g. 228 matches for the 380-fixture prod PL season), while Elo and Poisson are warmed on all 304 pre-test matches (train+val). This is an asymmetry of the training regime, not the test set. Expanding-window online LR is a future enhancement, explicitly out of scope here.
- McNemar p-values use the exact two-sided binomial test on accuracy discordance. Bootstrap CIs use 2000 resamples (default) with the percentile method and a zero-difference p-value derived from the CI.
- No model is ranked as 'superior'; metrics are descriptive.
