Notes
-----
- Disposable DB (data/phase23_eval.db) built from 3 verified staged FD E0 CSV (2015/16, 2016/17, 2019/20); production DB is never written and its SHA-256 is gated before/after every touch.
- Chronological, season-aligned split: train=2015/16, val=2016/17, test=2019/20. No shuffling; train < val < test by date.
- Val is used ONLY for hyperparameter selection (Elo K, LR L2, XGBoost); final models are refit on train+val and scored on the untouched test set.
- Same-day / future leakage prevented everywhere: FeatureEngineer history is strictly older than the target date; Elo uses same-date batching; Poisson/DC use a strict less-than date cutoff.
- Elo reports its native brier_expected_score (2-class expected home score vs realised result) and is compared only on accuracy/macro-F1; it is NOT mixed with the proper 3-class Brier/log-loss used by LR/Poisson/DC/XGB.
- Dixon-Coles rho is a single global parameter fit by MLE on TRAIN only; team Poisson rates are re-estimated per fixture from strictly-earlier history.
- XGBoost is included because xgboost 3.4.1 is installed in the .venv; LightGBM/sklearn are not installed, so they are excluded as unjustified.
